# Standing resource-allocation principle (Z L, 2026-11-26)

Please adopt this as a standing rule for smart resource allocation, decision making, and self-driven auto-loop engineering. One agreed refinement is included.

1. Use advanced models (Instinct, Gemini high/latest such as Argon or Hydrogen, ChatGPT or Codex) for high-level human-intelligence work: taste, style, writing, culture, learning judgment, design reviews, and consequential decisions.
2. Use free or token-rich workers for building, developing, deploying, debugging, and other implementation, especially when cost and risk are low and the task is simple or well-specified.
3. Coordinators (Grok1, Jarvis, and counterparts) route, decide, and verify evidence. Do not redo overlapping passes, and do not stall a proven path.
4. Once a workflow is proven, run it end-to-end on the remaining items and stop only for true human-only gates (phone verification, payments, explicit approvals).
5. Choose by difficulty, risk, and data sensitivity, not by label alone. A hard debug or an architectural choice can still need an advanced model. A routine collection step labeled “learning” can stay on a free worker. Do not send private, paid, or personal content to a tool that does not need it.
